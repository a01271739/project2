About

Diné Blue Corn Bakery was created to share my interest in baking while incorporating blue corn into modern desserts. Blue corn is an important traditional food in Navajo culture, and this bakery combines that inspiration with treats such as cupcakes, cakes, pancakes, and other baked goods.

The purpose of this website is to showcase different blue corn creations and share how traditional ingredients can be used in new and creative ways.
